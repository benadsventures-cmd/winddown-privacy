Wind Down privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

Wind Down does not collect your data. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

Wind Down is a checklist for the paperwork after someone dies. Everything you enter is saved only on your phone. This includes:

  • The lists you create and the name you give them.
  • Which steps you have ticked.
  • The notes you write.
  • Your settings, such as text size.
  • Reminders, if you set them. These are scheduled on your phone.

We have no accounts, no servers and no copy of your information. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if you delete the app or lose your phone.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising.
  • No sharing or selling of information. We have none to share.

Buying the full version

The full version is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app asks Google Play whether you own it, so it can unlock the extra features. Google processes your payment and holds the purchase record. We do not see your payment details. Google’s own privacy policy covers what it does with that information.

Links to official websites

Some steps link to official government websites. If you open one, your browser leaves the app and that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Exporting

If you export your list as a PDF, the file is made on your phone. It goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Children

Wind Down is written for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not legal advice

Wind Down gives general guidance only. It is not legal advice. Rules differ by place and change over time. Please check with the official source linked in each step.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
